Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

About the application

ISART Telegram PhotoBot helps users organize photos received through Telegram and upload them to an authorized Google Drive account.

Information processed

The bot processes Telegram user identifiers, uploaded photos, and selected country, city and store information needed to organize the photos.

Google Drive access

The application uses Google Drive access to create folders and upload photos. Google account authorization is handled through Google OAuth.

Data storage

Photos are stored in the authorized Google Drive account. Organizational information is stored in the bot's database.

Data sharing

The application does not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Data is processed through Telegram, the application hosting provider, and Google Drive as required for the service.

Data deletion

Users may request deletion of their data by contacting the application administrator.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the application administrator through the support email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
